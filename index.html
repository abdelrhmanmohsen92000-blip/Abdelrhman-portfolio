I work at the point where architectural intent has to survive contact with construction — translating design decisions into coordinated, buildable Revit models at LOD 350 and above, and keeping architecture, structure, and MEP disciplines talking to each other before conflicts reach site.

My background spans full BIM lifecycles: from master planning and facade design, through clash-free multidisciplinary coordination, to construction-ready shop drawings, automated BOQ extraction, and statutory municipal licensing. I'm comfortable leading that process on large developments or handling it end-to-end for private clients as an independent architect.
